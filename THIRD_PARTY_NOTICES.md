## jcodec

- Project: https://github.com/jcodec/jcodec
- Version used: 0.2.5
- License: FreeBSD
- License page: http://jcodec.org/lic.html

jcodec provides MP4/M4A demuxing, H.264 video encoding/decoding, PCM/AAC
container support, and MP3 audio decoding used by the recording, import,
export and playback features.

## JAAD (Java Advanced Audio Decoder)

- Project: https://jaadec.sourceforge.net/
- License: Public Domain

JAAD provides AAC decoding. It is bundled by jcodec-javase and accessed through
jcodec's AACDecoder wrapper.
